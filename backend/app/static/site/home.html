Questions

Does browsing cost anything?

No. Searching and filtering our database is included with a free account. Only live searches you run yourself use credits.

How are credits priced?

A live search costs what it costs us to run (the web search plus reading the posts) plus a small margin. You see the exact price before you run it, and nothing is charged if a search fails.

How fresh are the jobs?

Public feeds are checked several times a day. The home page shows when the database last updated successfully.

Does Clientfinder contact the people posting?

No. We link you to the original post. You decide who to contact and what to say.

Is my account private?

Your searches and credits are only visible to you. Jobs found by any search are added to the shared database.
